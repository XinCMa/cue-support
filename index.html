Get help with Cue, report a problem, or suggest a feature.

Contact

Email xinchunma226@gmail.com. You can also open Report & Feedback in Cue’s Settings.

For a problem report, include your device model, iOS version, Cue version, and what happened. A screenshot can help explain the issue. Share only information you are comfortable sending.

Apple Music and playback

Cue uses Apple Music to play songs. Full catalog playback requires an active Apple Music subscription. Check that Apple Music plays on your device and that Cue has permission to access your music.

Importing playlists

Large playlists can take time to analyze. If an import pauses, return to Cue so it can continue. Song analysis availability varies, and some songs may have no matching feature data.

Cue Pro

To manage or cancel Cue Pro on iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and choose Cue. For help with access after a purchase, contact the email above.
